# Code of Conduct

AXPilot adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The canonical text at that link is the binding version; it is incorporated
here by reference. Translations are listed at
[contributor-covenant.org/translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/).

The summary below is for convenience only. Where it differs from the canonical text, the canonical
text applies.

## In short

- **Our pledge.** We want participation in AXPilot to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
  regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
  gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
  nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
  orientation.
- **Expected behavior.** Be respectful and empathetic, accept constructive feedback gracefully,
  give it kindly, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community.
- **Unacceptable behavior.** Sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory
  comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing someone's
  private information without their explicit permission, and other conduct that would be
  inappropriate in a professional setting.
- **Scope.** All project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat), and public
  spaces where someone is officially representing the project.

Until that address is set, you can contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.
Please do not report conduct issues in a public issue.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and community leaders will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Community leaders follow the Contributor Covenant's
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines),
which define four escalating consequences: **correction**, **warning**, **temporary ban** and
**permanent ban**, depending on the impact and repetition of the behavior.
